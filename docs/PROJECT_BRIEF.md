# PROJECT_BRIEF.md — PRECASTPRO Website

## Brand
- **Company**: PRECASTPRO™
- **Industry**: Precast concrete manufacturing, construction, infrastructure
- **Visual Identity**: Red + charcoal logo featuring a concrete hollow-core plank, bold industrial typography
- **Tone**: Professional, authoritative, technical, trustworthy
- **Language**: Professional American English

## Brand Colors (sampled from logo)
- Primary red: `#C8102E`
- Deep brand red: `#A50E1B`

- Light gray background concrete texture reference

- Civil engineers and structural engineers
- Architects specifying precast concrete
- Municipal and government infrastructure planners
- Developers (commercial, industrial, residential)
